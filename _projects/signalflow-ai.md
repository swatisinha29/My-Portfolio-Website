> **Guiding principle:** AI investigates and recommends; the PM reviews and decides.

## What SignalFlow does

SignalFlow turns noisy customer feedback into defensible recommendations that a PM can review.


1. **Analyzes customer feedback** to find recurring problems.
2. **Investigates supporting evidence** for each problem.
3. **Surfaces prioritized product opportunities** for the PM to review.

## How the agent is built

- **Tool-based investigation.** The agent uses tools to dig into the feedback rather than answering from a single prompt.
- **Evidence extraction.** Each opportunity is backed by the feedback that supports it.
- **Deterministic prioritization logic.** Ranking follows explicit rules, so the result is consistent and explainable.
- **Human-in-the-loop decisions.** The PM reviews every recommendation and makes the final call.

## What I owned

I owned the product end to end: the user flows, the agent's behavior, the prioritization logic, the guardrails and the overall product experience, from concept to a working prototype.
