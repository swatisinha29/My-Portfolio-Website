> **In one sentence:** businesses collect data through cookies but have no system to obtain, manage and enforce consent. CookieX became that system, and a free compliance scanner turned awareness of the problem into a self-serve acquisition funnel.

## The problem

Businesses collect user data through cookies but lack systems to obtain, manage and enforce user consent. That creates compliance risk and erodes user trust.

- There was no centralized consent system.
- Regulatory pressure was rising, in India and globally.
- Many businesses did not know they were non-compliant.

## Vision

Build a **single source of truth for user consent**, so businesses can stay compliant while users keep control over their data.

## Users

| User | What they need |
| --- | --- |
| Businesses | To be compliant, and to manage consent in one place |
| End users | Control over their data, and transparency about how it is used |

## Go-to-market

We launched in India, aligned with the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA)**, with a roadmap to expand globally.

1. **Phase 1:** India launch.
2. **Phase 2:** global expansion.

## Core product flow

1. **User onboarding**
2. **Integration** (on WordPress, a 1-click plugin)
3. **Cookie scanning engine**
4. **Consent layer**
5. **Reporting**

## Key features

- Consent management system
- Cookie scanning engine
- Customizable cookie banner
- Multilingual support
- Compliance reporting
- Grievance management system

## Growth engine: the compliance scanner

Privacy products have an awareness problem: businesses don't buy a fix for a risk they cannot see. So we built a **free compliance scanner**.

1. The user enters a domain.
2. The system scans the website.
3. It shows a risk level (high, medium or low) and a breakdown of trackers.
4. It prompts the user to act, and converts them to a paid plan.

**Why it worked:** it created awareness, built urgency and delivered value instantly.

## Execution

- Built platform-first, with product-led growth as the acquisition model.
- Prioritized backend systems before UI, and designed a modular architecture.
- Balanced compliance with usability.
- Sequenced the build as **scanner → CMP → multilingual → grievance**.
- Redesigned activation with guided setup, compliance templates and automated cookie categorization, which raised onboarding completion from **40% to 85%**.
- Cut WordPress integration time from **10 minutes to 2 minutes** with a 1-click plugin.
- Launched consent lifecycle capabilities: audit logs, preference management, analytics dashboards and grievance workflows.

## Key product decisions

- **API and system first**
- **India-first strategy**
- **Product-led growth through the scanner**
- **A configurable system**

## Challenges

## Impact

- Enabled structured consent management for businesses.
- Reduced compliance risk.
- Created a self-serve acquisition funnel.

## What I learned

- Consent is a system, not a feature.
- Compliance products need a strong go-to-market, not just functionality.
- Awareness drives adoption in privacy products.
- Localization, of both language and regulation, is critical.
- Platform thinking beats feature thinking.

## If I rebuilt it today

- **Deeper integrations:** Shopify, Webflow and other CMS tools.
- **Smart optimization:** AI-driven consent UX and dynamic banner personalization.
